BOSS GUIDE

Defeat every boss with detailed attack patterns and proven strategies.

🤖

GLITCH GUARD

Level 1 - Tutorial Mini-Boss

HP
500
Easy

Attack Patterns

Glitch Slash

Telegraphed melee combo. Dodge backward.

Pixel Storm

Ranged pixel bullets. Keep moving.

Strategy

Tutorial boss. Stay at mid-range, punish after each attack combo. No special mechanics. Use your starting weapon.

⚔️

NEON SAMURAI

Level 2 - First Real Boss

HP
1,200
Normal

Attack Patterns

Blade Dance

3-hit sword combo. Dash through last hit.

Shadow Step

Teleports behind you. Check reflections.

Phase 2 - Iai Strike

One-hit kill slash. Hide behind pillars.

Strategy

Fight in open areas. Watch for sword trail before Shadow Step. Phase 2 requires cover - use pillars. Consistent DPS wins.

⚙️

ASSEMBLY LINE GUARDIAN

Level 3 - Industrial Boss

HP
2,500
Hard

Attack Patterns

Conveyor Crush

Floor extends, push to edges. Jump.

Drill Rush

Charging attack. Wall jump to avoid.

Summon Drones

Spawns 4 patrol bots. Kill quickly.

Strategy

Stay mobile, avoid conveyor floor. Melee weapons excel during Drill Rush recovery. Prioritize drones to reduce chaos.

👁️

THEIA - GOD OF VANITY

Level 4 - Final Boss

HP
8,000
EXTREME

Attack Patterns

Mirror Rain

Shatters into 12 projectiles. Find gaps.

Vanity Blast

Charged AoE. Keep distance.

Clone Strike

4 clones fire lasers. Real one glows.

Phase 2 - Reflection

Fight your clone. Hit when shields down.

Phase 3 - Final Form

All attacks faster. Hatchmon essential.

Strategy

• Use ranged weapons for Clone Strike
• Phase 2: Hit only when glows
• Phase 3: Hatchmon heals you
• Stack HP items before this fight

Recommended: Full weapon upgrades, 20+ items, evolved Hatchmon
